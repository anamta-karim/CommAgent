# Community Guidelines & Eligibility — Women in Tech Community

## Eligibility
Open to women and non-binary individuals in tech roles or transitioning into
tech (students, career-switchers, early-career professionals). No prior
coding experience required for the beginner track; mid/senior track requires
1+ years professional experience.

## Code of Conduct
- Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
- No self-promotion or unsolicited recruiting in community channels without
  admin approval.
- Keep mentor/mentee conversations confidential unless both parties agree to
  share publicly.
- Violations are reviewed by the community team; first offense = warning,
  repeat offense = removal.

## Pricing & Plans
- **Free tier**: access to group sessions, Telegram community, resource hub.
- **Mentorship tier** ($15/month): includes 2 monthly 1:1 mentor sessions.
- **Career-track tier** ($40/month): includes weekly 1:1 sessions, mock
  interviews, and resume reviews.
- Students with a valid .edu email get 50% off any paid tier.

## Cancellation
Cancel anytime from account settings; access continues until the end of the
current billing cycle. No cancellation fees.
