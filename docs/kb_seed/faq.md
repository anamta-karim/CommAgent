# FAQ — Women in Tech Community

**Q: How do I get access to the mentorship program?**
Fill the intake form on our website. You'll be matched with a mentor within
5 working days and added to the private Telegram group.

**Q: I didn't receive my welcome email, what do I do?**
Check spam first. If it's not there after 24 hours, message @community_admin
on Telegram with your registered email.

**Q: How do I report a bug in the community platform/app?**
Post in #tech-issues on Slack, or DM @community_admin with a screenshot and
steps to reproduce. Critical bugs are triaged within 24 hours.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I can't continue the program?**
Yes, full refund within the first 7 days of joining. Email
billing@womenintech-community.org with your registration ID. After 7 days,
refunds are prorated based on sessions attended.

**Q: How do I change my mentor if it's not a good fit?**
Message @community_admin — no questions asked, we'll rematch you within 3
working days.

**Q: Are sessions recorded if I miss one?**
Yes, all group sessions are recorded and posted in the Notion resource hub
within 24 hours. 1:1 mentor sessions are not recorded by default.
